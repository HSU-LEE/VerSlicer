VerSlicer

This installer puts VerSlicer in your Applications folder.

  1. Click Continue, then Install.
  2. When it finishes, open VerSlicer from Applications.

If macOS still says VerSlicer is damaged or can’t be opened, run this once in Terminal (select it and press ⌘C):

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VerSlicer.app
